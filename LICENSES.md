# Licensing

ProEQ uses two licences, split by what the files are.

| Material | Licence |
|----------|---------|
| Hardware design files: KiCad projects, symbol and footprint libraries, BOM and manufacturing data, mechanical and CAD deliverables | CERN Open Hardware Licence Version 2 - Weakly Reciprocal (CERN-OHL-W-2.0) |
| Firmware in `firmware/` | MIT |

The full CERN-OHL-W-2.0 text is in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). The upstream text used
is <https://gitlab.com/ohwr/project/cernohl/-/wikis/uploads/82b567f43ce515395f7ddbfbad7a8806/cern_ohl_w_v2.txt>.

The MIT text covering the firmware is in [`firmware/LICENSE`](firmware/LICENSE).
